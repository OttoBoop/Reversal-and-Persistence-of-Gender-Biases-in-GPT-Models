# Journal Style Research

Style-and-fit research for the paper *Reversal and Persistence of Gender Biases in GPT
Models*, comparing its current style (Public Choice / Motoki template — see
[`00_baseline_your_paper_profile.md`](00_baseline_your_paper_profile.md)) against four
candidate journals. Each dossier studies the journal's **author guidelines (style guide)**
*and* **examples of recently published papers**, then documents how that journal's style
**differs from this paper** and what adaptation it would require.

> Scope per the request: **style comparison + fit recommendation** (what would change,
> adaptation effort). Submission logistics (APC, timelines, acceptance rates) are out of
> scope. Dossiers are written in English.

## How to read

1. Start with `00` to recall the baseline (what your paper *is*, stylistically).
2. Read each journal dossier (`01`–`04`): **Snapshot → Style guide → Published exemplars
   → How it differs from your paper → Fit & recommendation**.
3. Finish with `05` for the side-by-side matrix and the overall ranking.

*Each dossier cites the journal's actual guidelines (with URLs) and names real example
papers. Any guideline point that could not be confirmed from a primary source is marked
`UNCONFIRMED`.*
